FAQ

How many business brokers are there in California?

The Broker Index currently lists 48 business brokers operating in California. Coverage grows as brokers and members request listings.

How do I check a broker's reputation in California?

Look them up here for client ratings, ask for references from recently closed deals in your size range, and confirm relevant credentials (CBI, M&AMI) and any state license requirements. Our guide to vetting an M&A broker walks through the full checklist.

How much do business brokers charge in California?

The same national patterns apply in California: 8–12% success fees under $2M (10% typical), Double Lehman tiers above that, and $10k–$25k minimum fees. Full breakdown in our fee guide.

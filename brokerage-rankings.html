Best business brokerages

Ranked on firm score (70%), consistency across agents (20%) and review coverage (10%). A firm needs reviews across at least scored agents to be ranked. Full methodology

Firm score

Volume-weighted average of every scored agent's composite, out of 10.

Consistency

How close the firm's best and worst agents are. You get assigned an agent, not an average.

Coverage

Share of the firm's agents with enough reviews to be scored. Stops two rainmakers carrying fifteen unknowns.
